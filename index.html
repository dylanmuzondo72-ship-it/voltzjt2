Artist Biography

Voltz JT is the stage name of Nkosilathi Sibiya, a Zimbabwean hip-hop artist born on 19 March 1999. He is associated with Chitungwiza and has been active since 2016, building a catalogue that connects street experience, friendship, ambition, pressure and the realities facing young Zimbabweans.

His music stands out because it speaks in a language and tone that feels close to home. Instead of presenting hip-hop as a borrowed image, Voltz JT gives it Zimbabwean identity through delivery, storytelling and visuals that match the culture around him.

Sound & Identity

Voltz JT’s sound sits inside Zimbabwean hip-hop with drill energy, Shona expression, melodic hooks and hard social observation. Songs such as Shamwari Yangu, These Days, Mukoma Brian and material from Life Of Muvhimi and N.O.P Makoni helped build his position as one of the most recognisable voices in the scene.
